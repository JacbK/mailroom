Gmail Switchboard

All your Gmail accounts.
One local MCP.

Search, read, draft, send, label, archive, and trash email from Codex or Claude. Add as many Gmail accounts as you want.

It stays on your Mac.

OAuth tokens live in macOS Keychain. The MCP runs over local stdio. There is no hosted backend, email database, analytics, or telemetry.

Every account stays obvious.

Search one account or all of them. Every message, draft, label, and thread ID includes its account so an action cannot silently land in the wrong inbox.

Writes are intentional.

Sending, forwarding, labeling, archiving, and trashing only happen when you ask. Delete means Gmail Trash, never permanent deletion.
