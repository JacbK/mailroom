Mailroom by Jacob Kieser

Privacy

This is a side project. I did not want email sitting on some random server, so it does not.

Last updated July 18, 2026

Google Limited Use

Mailroom's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Short version

Mailroom runs locally. No hosted backend, no email database, no ads, no analytics, no telemetry, and no selling your data.

What it can access

You can let it search and read messages, manage labels, make and update drafts, send and forward email, archive, and move messages to Trash. It also reads the account email so it can tell inboxes apart.

What leaves your Mac

When you ask Codex, Claude, or another MCP client to work with an email, the email needed for that request goes to the client you picked. Their privacy terms and settings apply. Mailroom does not use Gmail data for ads, profiling, sale, or AI model training.

What gets saved

The Mailroom Google app ID ships with the plugin. It cannot access your account by itself. Your OAuth tokens go in macOS Keychain. Account names and non-secret config stay on your Mac. Requested email can still exist in your local agent session while a task runs.

Removing it

Run ./setup --uninstall to remove the plugin and choose whether to revoke its tokens, or disconnect one account with ./mailroom auth remove <alias>. You can also revoke Mailroom from your Google Account security settings.
