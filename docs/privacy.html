Privacy, basically

Last updated July 17, 2026

Short version

This is a side project that runs locally. No backend, no email database, no ads, no analytics, no telemetry, and no selling your data.

What it can access

You can authorize it to search and read messages, manage labels, create and update drafts, send and forward email, archive messages, and move messages to Trash. It also reads the connected email address so it can tell your accounts apart.

What leaves your Mac

When you ask Codex, Claude, or another MCP client to work with email, the email needed for that request goes to the client you picked. That client and its model provider have their own privacy terms and settings. Switchboard does not use Gmail data for ads, profiling, sale, or AI model training.

What gets saved

OAuth tokens go in macOS Keychain. Account names and non-secret config stay on your Mac. Emails are not stored on a Switchboard server because there is no server. Requested email can still exist in your local agent session while a task runs.

Removing it

Disconnect the account and remove its local token. You can also revoke access from your Google Account security settings. Either way, future access stops.

Google's rules

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
