Privacy

Last updated July 17, 2026

What the app accesses

Switchboard by Jacob Kieser, used by the Gmail Switchboard plugin, asks for Gmail access so you can search and read messages, manage labels, create and update drafts, send and forward email, archive messages, and move messages to Trash. It also reads the connected account address so accounts can be identified correctly.

How your data is used

Your Gmail data is used only to perform the Gmail actions you request. Gmail Switchboard does not use it for advertising, profiling, sale, or training AI models.

When you ask Codex, Claude, or another MCP client to work with email, the requested Gmail data is returned to that client so it can complete your request. That client and its model provider handle the data under their own privacy terms and settings.

Where data is stored

OAuth tokens are stored in your macOS Keychain. Account aliases and non-secret configuration are stored locally on your Mac. Gmail content is not stored on a Gmail Switchboard server because there is no hosted backend. Requested content may exist transiently in your local MCP and client session while a task runs.

Sharing

Gmail Switchboard does not sell your data or share it with advertisers or data brokers. It only transfers the data needed to the MCP client you chose, when you explicitly request a Gmail feature, or when required by law.

Control and deletion

You can disconnect an account from Gmail Switchboard and remove its local token at any time. You can also revoke access from your Google Account security settings. Removing the local token ends future access.

Google API data

Gmail Switchboard’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
